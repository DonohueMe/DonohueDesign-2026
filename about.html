About me

I'm Jim Donohue and owner of Donohue Design

With a strong background in user experience (UX) design, I have held full-time positions at leading San Francisco Bay Area companies such as Apple, Wells Fargo, Deloitte, and Intel Security.

In addition to my corporate roles, I've been freelancing through Donohue Design since 1995 — 30+ years building brands, websites, and marketing systems for clients. Throughout this time, I've collaborated with local businesses, startups, and numerous advertising and marketing agencies across Santa Rosa and the North Bay.

How I approach the work

Good design starts with a question: what do you need this website to do? Not how should it look — what should it accomplish.

Every page, every call-to-action, and every navigation path is a decision. I map those decisions to your business goals before a single pixel gets placed. The result is a site that isn't just attractive — it's built to convert.

From information architecture and content hierarchy to mobile performance and SEO structure, strategy is woven into every layer of the build.
