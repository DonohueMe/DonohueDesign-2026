Website development

Most small-business websites are built on page builders and bloated WordPress themes. I write clean, hand-coded sites that load fast, stay secure, and are easy for any developer to maintain.

Design and development live under one roof — so what gets approved in the mockup is exactly what ships in production. No plugin surprises, no mystery dependencies, no rebuild when you outgrow a template.

I'm Jim Donohue, based in Santa Rosa, CA. I've spent 20+ years building digital products at companies like Wells Fargo and Deloitte. Now I bring that same engineering discipline to websites for Sonoma County businesses.

Squarespace websites

Squarespace is a solid fit when you need a polished marketing site fast — with built-in hosting, SSL, and templates you can actually maintain. I design and build Squarespace sites that go beyond the defaults: custom layouts, brand-aligned styling, and conversion-focused page structure.

I also help businesses already on Squarespace — redesigns, performance fixes, SEO cleanup, and custom code injections when the platform's limits need a push.

WordPress websites

WordPress powers a huge share of the web for good reason — flexible, familiar, and easy for your team to update. I build lean WordPress sites with custom themes or carefully chosen foundations, not bloated multipurpose templates loaded with plugins you'll never use.

Security hardening, speed optimization, and a clean admin experience are part of every build. You get a site that's stable, findable, and straightforward to hand off to any WordPress developer down the road.

Shopify

Selling online means checkout has to work flawlessly — on every device, every time. I design and develop Shopify stores with clear product presentation, trust-building layout, and a purchase flow that reduces friction from first click to confirmation.

Theme customization, app integration, and storefront SEO are handled with the same strategy-first approach I bring to every project — so your store looks professional and converts, not just lists products.

No page builders. No lock-in.

You own your domain, hosting account, and all site files. I register domains in your name and document logins so you are never trapped on a proprietary platform.

Clean, maintainable code you can hand off to any developer — with version control, documented structure, and no mystery plugins holding the site together.

From design approval to launch

Development follows a clear path: responsive build, content integration, cross-browser testing, performance audit, and launch with analytics wired up. Typical marketing sites take 6–10 weeks from kickoff to go-live.

Optional ongoing support covers updates, security monitoring, and content changes — so your site stays fast and current after launch day.
