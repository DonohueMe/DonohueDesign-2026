Conversational AI agents

Most visitors have questions before they're ready to call. A conversational AI agent answers them instantly — in plain language, pulled from your services, FAQs, pricing guidance, and policies.

I build knowledge-base agents for Santa Rosa and Sonoma County businesses that turn passive website traffic into real conversations. Not a generic chat widget pasted onto a template — your business knowledge, your tone, your sales process, available 24/7.

I'm Jim Donohue. I've spent 20+ years building digital products at companies like Wells Fargo and Deloitte. Now I deploy AI agents that work the way your best employee would — if they never slept and never forgot a detail.

Trained on your knowledge base

Off-the-shelf chatbots hallucinate. A knowledge-base agent is different — it retrieves answers from content you control and approve. Service areas, warranties, turnaround times, what to expect on a first visit, how pricing works.

I structure that knowledge from your website, brochures, intake forms, and internal docs — then connect it to an agent that knows when to answer, when to ask a clarifying question, and when to hand off to a human. The result feels like talking to someone who actually works there.

Qualify leads in conversation

Conversation is only half of it. A good agent captures intent and acts on it. A visitor asks about availability — the agent collects their name, email, and project details, then routes a summary to your inbox or CRM. A hot lead comes in after hours — you get notified immediately, and they get a confirmation that sets expectations.

Lead capture stops being a contact form at the bottom of a page. It becomes an always-on system: qualify, capture, follow up, and report — so you spend time on prospects who are actually ready to buy.

On-brand tone and guardrails

Your agent should sound like your business, not a robot reading a manual. I tune response style, vocabulary, and escalation rules so the conversation stays professional, helpful, and on-message — even when visitors ask unexpected questions.

Clear boundaries matter too. The agent knows what it can answer, what requires a human, and what it should never say. You stay in control of the experience without babysitting every chat.

Embedded where your customers already are

Agents deploy on your website, landing pages, and client portals — wherever questions tend to stall a sale. Integrations with email, CRM, and notification tools mean conversations don't die in a chat window. Every qualified lead lands where your team already works.

Whether you're a service business fielding the same five questions daily or a professional practice routing intake before a consultation, the agent fits your workflow — not the other way around.

From knowledge audit to live agent

Every deployment follows a clear path: audit your content, structure the knowledge base, configure the agent's tone and escalation rules, test with real scenarios, and go live with analytics wired up. Typical projects take 3–6 weeks depending on content volume and integration complexity.

Ongoing tuning keeps the agent sharp as your services, pricing, and FAQs evolve — so answers stay accurate long after launch day.
