Website development

A good strategy means nothing if the build falls apart. Website development is where your plan becomes a real site that loads fast and does its job. I build it myself — every page, every function — on whatever platform fits your business best.

Your site needs to work on every screen. Mobile-first development isn’t optional anymore. Most people in Santa Rosa search from their phones, whether they’re looking for a lunch spot near Railroad Square or a contractor out in Rincon Valley. If your site loads slow or looks broken on a phone, they’re gone.

Here’s what website development covers in my process:

  • Custom design, whether hand-built, Squarespace, or WordPress — the right platform for your needs and budget
  • Mobile-first layouts tested on real devices, not just previews
  • Fast-loading pages, optimized for how people actually browse on mobile
  • Cross-browser testing, so the site works the same on every device and browser

And this is the foundation everything else runs on — the AI receptionist, booking tools, and lead capture only work as well as the code underneath them. Get the build right, and the rest holds up.
