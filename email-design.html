Email Design

Email is still one of the highest-ROI channels in marketing — when the design earns the open, the message reads fast, and the call to action is impossible to miss. A cluttered template or a broken layout on mobile kills the campaign before it starts.

I design email campaigns and newsletters for Santa Rosa and Sonoma County businesses — templates built for Mailchimp, Constant Contact, HubSpot, or your ESP of choice. On-brand, mobile-friendly, and ready to send. Not generic drag-and-drop blocks pasted together. Campaigns that look like they came from a company worth doing business with.

I'm Jim Donohue — a designer with 20+ years of experience in brand identity, marketing collateral, and digital campaigns for companies from local contractors to national financial institutions.

Designed to convert, not just deliver

A strong email campaign has a clear job: get opened, communicate one main idea, and move people to act — click, call, book, buy. Subject line and preview text do the selling before the email is opened. Inside, hierarchy does the rest: headline, supporting copy, visual proof, and a single focused CTA.

Before design begins, I want to understand the campaign goal — welcome series, seasonal promotion, event invite, nurture sequence, re-engagement — and who’s on the list. That shapes tone, length, imagery, and how aggressive the ask should be.

How email design works

Every project starts with your content, audience, and send platform — what you need to say, who receives it, and where it will live. Then concept development: layout options that balance readability, brand consistency, and how the email renders across clients and devices.

We refine together — adjusting headlines, image placement, button copy, and mobile stacking until the email reads cleanly everywhere. You approve the final direction before files are handed off or loaded into your ESP.

What you receive

You get production-ready email files — HTML templates tested across common clients, or native templates built inside your platform. Formats cover one-off campaigns, recurring newsletters, automated drip sequences, and reusable modular blocks you can mix for future sends.

Typography stays on-brand, images are sized for fast load, and CTAs are built for thumbs on a phone screen. Need matching landing pages or social assets to support the campaign? I can handle the full set so everything looks like it belongs together.

Email campaigns plus everything around them

Email works best as part of a consistent marketing system — website, social, print, and automation working toward the same message. I handle the design layer so your campaigns look cohesive from the inbox to the page people land on afterward.

Whether you need a single launch email or a template library your team can reuse for years, we'll build something you're proud to send.
